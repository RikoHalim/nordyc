Read `AGENTS.md`, `STATUS.md`, `docs/21_PHASE4_COURSE_RELEVANCE.md`, `docs/22_CAREER_ALIGNMENT_AND_PATHWAYS_PLAN.md`, architecture/analysis/retrieval specifications, the Phase 3 runbook/report, workforce methodology, test plan and expected fixture behaviors. Preserve prior APIs, data and offline fixtures. The latest Phase 5–6 enhancement request changes planning only; no later-phase implementation is authorized.

Preserve one durable analysis job for **every available course appearance in a selected programme**, using the existing upload-first catalogue. Keep extraction ambiguities and incomplete curriculum coverage visible. Do not require a detailed syllabus, infer taught content from titles or omit courses with weak evidence.

Retrieve PostgreSQL evidence first: cross-disciplinary occupational skills/tasks, applicable MQA/professional standards, dated workforce observations and independent AI task impacts. Apply deterministic relevance, quality, edition/date/freshness, identity, rights and completeness checks for each claim. Preserve Phase 3 distinctions between structured provisional values, unreviewed PDF candidates and engineering-reviewed cards.

Important missing/stale/insufficient evidence must automatically trigger bounded research when an approved search API/tool is enabled and configured. Prioritise authoritative original government, academic, professional and credible research publications. Use modular adapters, allowlists, rates/time/query/document budgets, the existing safe transport and private-data controls. Search snippets and unsupported model research are candidates only. Verify actual claims in original passages/tables, preserve URLs/dates/locators/allowed excerpts and provenance, and import/cache/version findings for reuse only where permissions allow. Recheck sufficiency; record failures/restrictions and manual instructions without bypassing access rules or substituting synthetic records.

Use **one explicitly configured Gemini model through the existing direct REST adapter** over the frozen admitted evidence packet; Brave is the approved search adapter. Both adapters exist, but actual credential-backed calls remain unverified. Configure/disclose the single actual model for later live testing. Do not train models, use Replit AI Integrations or add research agents/models. Keep offline fixtures and explicit missing-model behavior; never claim live analysis from mocked or deterministic output.

Produce qualitative course review findings and programme-level coverage questions with evidence class, source/version/reference IDs, locators/dates, rationale, unknowns and research status. Validate Pydantic/JSON-schema output and the final serialized findings for source support and policy compliance. Display **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE** (`insufficient_evidence`) for unsupported conclusions. Keep `needs_syllabus_confirmation` for title-based benchmarking and `is_final_curriculum_verdict=false`. Reject invented citations, unsupported quotations, obsolete/replacement verdicts and arbitrary percentages. Academic action generation/comparison belongs to Phase 5.

Consume the existing deterministic workforce radar independently from AI task evidence. Preserve rising/declining/stable/insufficient states, comparison populations, periods, method/threshold policy and source observations. Never turn broad-group changes into individual occupation/skill demand or decline into AI causation. AI transformation requires admissible observed task evidence and reviewed identity; exposure/prediction alone cannot activate it. Declining demand or high AI exposure cannot establish course obsolescence, taught content or missing curriculum topics.

Use immutable run manifests, permitted evidence cache/versioning and durable attempt reporting. Research refresh creates a new run; frozen replay makes no external requests. Add only necessary schema/API changes with migrations/tests, preserving version-local evidence constraints. Do not download everything at startup or expose unrestricted research/ingestion triggers.

Use mocked providers for offline tests and separately report an opt-in genuine permitted search → verify → import/query → finding smoke test with actual provider/model and source versions. Never equate fixture success with live integration.

Run targeted then full relevant tests, update STATUS with measured results/blockers, report changes and stop. Do not start Phase 5 or commit/push unless separately instructed.
