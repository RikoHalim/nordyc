# PHASE 5 — Explainable academic actions

Verify previous phases. Read `docs/07_ACADEMIC_ACTIONS.md`.

Given a programme analysis, propose the **least disruptive evidence-supported** primary academic intervention plus compare alternatives, always including `no_change`, with distinct `request_review` when evidence is weak. Support syllabus revision, redesign/consolidation, new course/elective and unified workshops & industry engagement (hands-on, seminars, speakers, talks). Use strong evidence gates for replacement/consolidation; title-only must not trigger a verified final decision. Human review statuses and assumptions included. Workshop attendance is not verified assessed competency.

Tests for intervention gating, evidence citations, no-change comparison, reviewer pending status, lack-of-data abstention. Update STATUS; STOP.
