# Preliminary demo walkthrough (working MVP; public or synthetic)

1. Start backend and UI; choose **XMUM**.
2. Show publicly discovered undergraduate programmes, with course counts and source URL per programme; show catalogue gaps clearly.
3. Open e.g. one computing, one business and one communication/science programme (select programmes actually verifiably available at demo time).
4. Run analysis **for an entire selected programme**, not one course at a time.
5. Explain title-only limit on a course card: `Review whether X is covered` not `X is absent`.
6. Show real dated occupation/AI source with original publisher and source; compare against a dated prior snapshot or an explicitly synthetic one.
7. View one AI-proposed academic action, evidence/uncertainty and comparison with `no_change` and a less disruptive workshop.
8. Change snapshot/scenario and show **traceably changed outcome** (without fake scores).
9. Show academic plan/draft and SDG rationale; reviewer status pending.
10. End with unresolved data/organiser-clarification risks, not a claim that all university course contents were audited.

## 30-second value proposition

"SHIFT//OS does not simply rank university courses as obsolete. It benchmarks available programme catalogues against traceable AI and workforce evidence, tells career centres which academic questions matter most, compares reasonable interventions and shows when a decision should be revisited."

## Questions the judges may ask

- **Why not just ChatGPT?** Versioned sources, conservative evidence gates, reproducible scenario replay, course-by-course provenance, human review.
- **How do you know a title is outdated?** We **don't**; we flag it for verification, not condemnation.
- **Is it every university?** Pilot XMUM; on-demand architecture for Malaysia, not nationwide pre-crawling.
- **Where are the personal reskilling and role-matching requirements?** Honest mapping and outstanding organiser waiver documented in compliance matrix; do not bluff.
