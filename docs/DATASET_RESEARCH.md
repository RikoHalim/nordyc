## Decision

A public-evidence preliminary MVP is feasible: discover the current XMUM directory, import every publicly exposed **Main Courses** list, and demonstrate conservative review prompts on a few diverse programmes. Public evidence is not sufficient for definitive obsolete/replacement decisions, a certified degree audit, skill-level Malaysian demand rankings, or claims about actual teaching and assessment.

The practical reference core is **XMUM + relevant MQA/MQF benchmarks + ESCO with Malaysian classification context + two DOSM workbooks + selected dated AI studies**. UN targets and a small learning-resource catalogue support hackathon compliance. MyNSR is useful local enrichment, but its verified pilot scope and unverified bulk export make it unsuitable as the sole taxonomy.

The requested files are provided in the outer workspace and copied into the working `shift-os/` repository. The outer starter's “not started” state must not erase the nested repository's completed Phase 01. Existing uncommitted backend changes were not modified.

## Verification method and how to use this inventory

Official publisher pages, document links, selected PDF sections and small API/data responses were checked. Research used bounded manual retrieval, not a university crawler. Large reports were not downloaded wholesale. The programme audit inspected 25 HTML course sections, resolved 25 official brochure links, and fully cross-checked three brochure course pages. Two DOSM workbooks were inspected in memory. ESCO search/resource responses and UN goal/target API responses were exercised. No verified-source record has been persisted in the application.

The authoritative structured inventory is [dataset_registry.csv](../data/dataset_registry.csv). Its 73 rows cover 25 programme sources plus directories, standards, taxonomies, dated releases, reports and supporting resources. A row is a source artifact/family, not a claim that 73 independent datasets have been acquired.

- **Verified:** stated source content/metadata was inspected. This does not certify every row, downstream mapping, current cohort, licence permission or production availability.
- **Partially verified:** authoritative metadata/link exists, but the underlying file, full contents, rights or structured export remains unverified. Read `endpoint_verification` and `limitations`.
- **Unverified:** inaccessible/unconfirmed candidate; no factual evidence may be admitted from it. The legacy XMUM route is explicitly recorded this way.
- `acquired_into_backend=No` applies to every registry row. Access, rights, automation policy and backend import are separate states.

Each row records the requested 15 dimensions: official name/publisher; exact source and download/API URLs; type/format; geography/discipline; available editions, newest verified publication/update and observation period; publisher cadence; licence/rights; registration/payment; relevance; missing fields; acquisition method; verification. It also records evidence subtype, quality, integration, reliability, reuse, maintenance and course-analysis limits. Empty download/API means **no verified endpoint**, not an invitation to construct one. “Unknown” is intentional. Dates in paths, upload timestamps, search-engine crawl dates and site copyright years are not publication dates. Proposed NORDYC polling intervals are in the acquisition plan, separately from publisher schedules.

MYFutureJobs/ILMIA dashboards may later add local detail, but no usable licensed bulk time series was verified here. Do not scrape job-board listings or make them an MVP dependency.

## E. AI capability, occupational impact and uncertainty

Use evidence cards, not a single disruption score. Each card needs publication date, observation/evaluation date, model/tool version where reported, setting, task, supported result, source locator and limitation. The existing `dated_ai_capability` class must be qualified by subtype: a forecast or exposure estimate must never display as measured capability.

ILO's report and the inspected HBS-hosted journal article have CC BY 4.0 notices. ILO's annex is extractable report content, **not a verified standalone CSV/API**. There is no verified comprehensive Malaysian course-to-AI-impact dataset. The proposed bridge from occupational task evidence to an academic review question is NORDYC inference. TCM clinical competency, marine fieldwork, language pedagogy and many discipline-specific practices need further evidence; absence is a valid result.

Direct GET tests returned **17 goals and 169 targets**. Goal fields include `code,title,description,uri`; target fields include `goal,code,title,description,uri,indicators`. The API's current text is not an official occupation/skill-to-SDG mapping. No dataset-specific unrestricted redistribution licence was established; review UN terms and keep original links. Bulk indicator time series would add little MVP value.

Proposed mappings must identify a specific mechanism: a renewable-energy analysis competency may support target [7.2](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal7); marine-pollution measurement may support [14.1](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal14); responsible public-interest source verification may support [16.10](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal16). These are **reviewable examples of inference**, not claims that a particular XMUM course teaches those competencies or produces measured SDG outcomes. UNESCO's 2017 ESD learning objectives offer supporting educational rationale, with full-file access still to confirm.

Ordering resources and matching them to a person's gap require additional logic and validation.

The reported CV-upload exemption does **not** confirm that individual skills intake, role matching or personalised pathways were waived. These sources support that compliance work; they do not establish that the institutional workflow fully satisfies it.

## Suitability and rejected expansion

High credibility is not sufficient: a source must add usable evidence at the right granularity. ESCO and DOSM are readily reusable structured references; XMUM/MQA provide authoritative but incomplete/unstructured evidence; MyNSR/MyMahir access and coverage prevent treating them as drop-in datasets. Research reports require narrow evidence extraction, not invented row-level data.

Defer full O*NET duplication, large university handbooks, full SDG indicator histories, nationwide curriculum collection, proprietary job-ad corpora and vendor leaderboards without task/model/method detail. They add maintenance or rights burdens without resolving title-only evidence. No source family removes the need for academic review.
